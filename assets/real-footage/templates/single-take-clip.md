# Single-take clip

Paste the chosen behavior's camera paragraph (`behaviors/<technique>.md`) into CAMERA and
fill its brackets. Drop the reference lines you do not use, and the dialogue lines in a
silent clip. Worked example: the fox whip-pan clip in spec Appendix A.

```text
GOAL: One continuous [N]-second clip that looks like unedited [device] footage shot by someone who is part of the scene. It exists to show one camera behavior: [behavior]. Clean full frame: no overlay, timestamp, REC indicator, battery icon, focus box, grid, border or subtitles. The camera and the person holding it never appear.

REFERENCES:
@[IMG1] supplies only [character A]'s [face and exact wardrobe]. Ignore its background, lighting and layout.
@[IMG2] supplies only [character B]'s [body, face and materials]. Ignore its background.
@[AUD1] supplies only [character A]'s voice. Use it for new lines in that voice, never as music or a replay of the recording.

CAST: Exactly [count] on screen: [list]. [Character A] is the only one wearing [signature garment]. Everyone else has a face and build clearly unlike [character A]. The operator is [where they stand or hide] and is never seen.

ACTION: [The setting in one sentence.] [Three to five short visible beats.] End on [a clear final image].

CAMERA: [The behavior's camera paragraph, fill-ins replaced: trigger, reaction, correction, settle, and its "not this" clause.]

SOUND: [Speaker] says [in what voice]: "[exact line]". The words are clear and intelligible, and lip movement stays in sync with them. [Room tone and action sounds.] [No music, unless the shot wants it.]

CONTINUITY: Keep cast count, faces, wardrobe, props, room layout and direction of action fixed. Perform the camera behavior once. Start when the action starts and end on the stated result.
```

Rules:
- One behavior per clip, performed once.
- Three to five beats in a clip of 10 seconds or less. Each beat is something visible.
- Every reference supplies only one thing and names what to ignore.
- Two voices in one clip: make them clearly different and say how.
- A short clip can run each block at a sentence. Keep all six headings anyway.
- No logos, brand names or readable text, unless Tony approves readable text for that shot.
  No real money or injuries, and no weapons in photoreal footage (a stylized game or cartoon
  weapon is fine).
- `@[IMG1]` is a stand-in tag. The engine swaps in the model's own syntax (guide section 13).
- Generate the 480p draft first. The 1080p final runs only after Tony passes the draft.
