# Voyeuristic

> Untested against a live generator. The failure modes are predictions; replace them with
> what the first runs show.

## Camera

```text
The operator stays hidden behind [COVER], filming through a narrow gap. Dark out-of-focus edges of [COVER] fill much of the foreground. When [CUE], the operator leans slowly sideways just far enough to reveal [ACTION], holds for a beat, then eases back behind cover until the gap narrows again and the frame comes to rest. [SUBJECTS] keep their eyes away from the lens and never notice. The operator stays put behind cover; this is not a camera roaming through the open room.
```

## Fill-ins
- `[COVER]`: the partly closed door, pillar, shelf or divider.
- `[CUE]`: what makes the operator lean (a laugh from the room, a sound of something
  being unwrapped).
- `[ACTION]`: what the lean reveals.
- `[SUBJECTS]`: who is being watched.

## Notes
- Keep it comic or harmless. Never use it on anyone in a private or vulnerable moment.
- The foreground cover is the proof. Say how much of the frame it fills.
