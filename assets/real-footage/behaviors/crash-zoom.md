# Crash zoom

> Untested against a live generator. The failure modes are predictions; replace them with
> what the first runs show.

## Camera

```text
Start genuinely wide, with [SUBJECTS] small in the room. The operator's feet stay planted. On [TRIGGER], the lens changes focal length fast into a medium shot of [SUBJECTS], magnifying the background with no camera travel. A brief radial smear, a slight overshoot and a moment of lost focus, then focus recovers on [FACES] and the frame settles there. Only the focal length moves: the operator does not step closer, and it is not an edit to a tighter framing.
```

## Fill-ins
- `[SUBJECTS]`: who the zoom lands on.
- `[TRIGGER]`: the surprise (a cork flies, someone falls off a chair, a robot speaks).
- `[FACES]`: whose faces focus finds at the end.

## Notes
- The wide start has to be truly wide or there is no zoom to see. Say how small the
  subjects are in the opening frame.
- Camcorder and documentary styles carry this best; a fisheye has no zoom.
