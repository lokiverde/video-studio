# Exposure shift

> Untested against a live generator. The failure modes are predictions; replace them with
> what the first runs show.

## Camera

```text
One uninterrupted handheld shot following [SUBJECT] from [DIM PLACE] into [BRIGHT PLACE]. The camera is exposed for the dark. As the operator crosses into the light, the image washes nearly white, then auto-exposure visibly pulls the brightness down over about a second until [SUBJECT] and [SCENERY] are both readable, and the frame settles on [FINAL VIEW]. The brightness change is the sensor adjusting live: not an edit, not a dissolve and not a grading pass.
```

## Fill-ins
- `[SUBJECT]`: who the operator follows.
- `[DIM PLACE]`: where it starts (a garage, a stairwell, a hallway).
- `[BRIGHT PLACE]`: where it ends (a sunny yard, a beach, a parking lot at noon).
- `[SCENERY]`: what becomes readable once exposure settles.
- `[FINAL VIEW]`: the closing frame.

## Notes
- Works with almost no dialogue. A short clip can run its six blocks at a line each.
- The washout is the proof. Say "nearly white", or the model plays it safe.
