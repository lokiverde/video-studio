# Frame block

> Untested against a live generator. The failure modes are predictions; replace them with
> what the first runs show.

## Camera

```text
Start looking across [SPACE] at [SUBJECT]. [CROSSER] passes once, close to the lens, carrying [NEAR OBJECT] that fills the near foreground and briefly hides [SUBJECT]. Autofocus grabs the nearby object and [SUBJECT] goes soft. Once the object clears, focus snaps back onto [SUBJECT]'s eyes and the operator reframes slightly upward, then holds. One viewpoint and one unbroken take: the focus change is the autofocus reacting, not an edit and not a deliberate focus pull.
```

## Fill-ins
- `[SPACE]`: the busy room (a break room, a kitchen, a garage).
- `[SUBJECT]`: who the camera is looking at.
- `[CROSSER]`: who walks between (a fictional coworker, a dog).
- `[NEAR OBJECT]`: what blocks the frame; bright or large reads best (a stack of pizza
  boxes with no printing, a white balloon).

## Notes
- Keep the near object blank: no labels, no logos, no readable print.
- The crosser never looks at the camera. Say so in CAST.
